## Selected provider candidate and bounded task

Candidate: [Runway Weather Oracle](https://runwayoracle.com/), `GET https://runwayoracle.com/v1/oracle/v3/ord-edge` (Chicago O'Hare/KORD). Its first-party page and machine catalog describe x402 payment in USDC on Base (`eip155:8453`) and list a $0.05 live-telemetry / $0.01 degraded-fallback price. The four catalog-declared free routes returned HTTP 200; the paid endpoint and NWS observation endpoint were not called. The free resources do **not** establish the paid endpoint's current operational status, response schema, signed result, stable request ID, or independently retrievable request log. The candidate remains **not qualified for execution**.

Proposed single-call task: request one KORD current-temperature nowcast and evaluate it against an independently captured official NWS KORD observation. Frozen rubric v1: response must be HTTP 200 and parse under the independently pinned response schema; contain KORD station identity, finite temperature and explicit units, and a source observation timestamp; source timestamp must be no more than 300 seconds old when the response is captured and no more than 30 seconds in the future relative to the trusted capture clock; the matching NWS observation must identify KORD, carry its original timestamp and units, and be no more than 15 minutes from the provider source timestamp; convert both values to Fahrenheit with a frozen standard conversion and require absolute difference ≤2°F. Preserve exact original response bytes and relevant headers, status and retrieval times; compute SHA-256; require a provider-issued request ID and independently verifiable provider-side delivery record bound to that response. Require an HTTP 200 and successful x402 settlement response, then independently verify the finalized Base transfer, payer, recipient, token contract and amount. The provider must be live-telemetry mode at the authorized quote. Any missing schema field, timestamp, unit, ID, evidence binding, or quote means no success. This rubric does not cure provider schema/proof fields that remain unknown; obtain and hash the schema before freezing an immutable run record.

The source overlap is disclosed: Runway states measurements use the exact NWS stations used for weather-market settlement. NWS is therefore not independent of the provider's source family. This experiment measures delivery and bounded contemporaneous agreement, **not independent forecast skill**. Before freeze, an independent reviewer must explicitly accept this data-lineage limitation for an E1 delivery demonstration. If rejected, replace the task/provider; do not weaken the rubric.

For outcome evidence, use the official [NWS API](https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api), station `KORD`, latest observations resource, with original observation payload, timestamp, headers, fetch clock, and SHA-256 retained. Respect NWS rate limits. Because both services use the same NWS station family, require reviewer acceptance before this can be presented as independent outcome provenance. Provider evidence must include the exact original request/response, status, relevant response headers, payment response, and a provider-originated request identifier or retrievable provider-side record that cryptographically or otherwise independently binds the delivery to that response. A client-generated hash and the Base transfer alone are not provider-side delivery proof. The run remains blocked until schema, provider identity and acceptable delivery proof are confirmed and frozen.

## Pre-execution freeze record

Before any external request, the operator and independent reviewer must sign a new immutable run record containing: cohort ID/version and this specification's Git commit/hash; isolated staging deployment and database schema; deterministic policy and code commit; operator and reviewer identities; exact task and expected deliverable; provider identity evidence; endpoint; response-schema digest; request-ID and delivery-proof methods; quote mode and exact request; allowed Base USDC proof profile; observation source and freshness bound; evaluation rubric and deadline; permitted artifacts; test issuer public trust registry; approved payer/recipient addresses; service price and network fee ceiling; per-call and aggregate caps; approval identity and expiry; stop conditions; and Decisions flag state. Any changed item requires a new version and approvals. The referenced [operator authorization packet](decisions-e1-authorization-packet-v1.md) is unsigned and authorizes nothing.

Proposed hard ceilings for a future separately approved run: one provider payment of at most **0.05 USDC on Base** for live telemetry; combined Base network/facilitator/wallet fees at most **$0.10 equivalent**; free staging Judgment at **$0.00**; maximum total **$0.15 equivalent including all fees**. This is not authorization. The provider's actual quote and recipient must be independently inspected before separate settlement approval. Base/USDC service settlement is distinct from the later RH/USDG paid-Judgment rail; E1 does not use or require RH/USDG. A segregated wallet and test issuer remain mandatory. No paid request, transaction, or payment has been performed.

## Cohort and causal sequence

| Case | Frozen expectation | Permitted receipt pattern | Evidence requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Positive real-service task | Fresh, reviewed, scoped facts support a small authorized task. | Genuine O, paid signed J, one externally executed X, then independently reviewed E in isolated staging. | Provider catalog/source capture; quote and payment proof; original signed X submission; finalized transfer; provider request ID and delivered result; rubric and independent outcome review. |
| Denied action | Known provider, policy, or budget veto. | O and, only if a separately approved assessment fee is paid, a `do_not_spend` J. No X or E. | Authentic negative facts and deterministic denial. A quote is not a paid J. |
| Insufficient evidence | Missing, stale, mismatched, or unverified observation. | Free `insufficient_evidence` response; O only if a genuine observation exists. No billable J, X, or E. | Missing/stale reason and zero-charge trace; no synthetic O to complete a chain. |

1. **Observation.** After approved staging and run freeze, capture the official NWS source artifact and retrieval time before assessment. A qualified reviewer records provider identity, task/intent binding, route, constraints, exact provider schema and quote, confidence basis, freshness expiry, and content digest. A catalog flag of `live` by itself is insufficient. The pre-spend model-visible packet contains only information available now; outcome data are withheld.
2. **Judgment.** Run the existing deterministic pre-spend policy in isolated staging with Decisions disabled for authorization. Record full normalized input, policy/config commit, evidence set, decision clock, returned decision and reason. Append a zero-charge staging J with `payment_required=false` only if the genuine evidence passes the deterministic policy. Independently replay from frozen inputs. No model suggestion can change evidence, budget, or ALLOW. Insufficient evidence remains free and does not create a billable J. RH/USDG is not required.
3. **Execution.** Only after separate approval for the Judgment settlement, provider request, and external payment, obtain the separately required execution capability and perform exactly one frozen request using the approved external payer. Keep original signed proof input; verify Base finalized USDC Transfer, exact payer/recipient/amount, request hash, judgment window, and uniqueness. The provider must return an independently inspectable request ID and response artifact; a transfer is not evidence of delivery. If execution is denied, timed out, over budget, or ambiguous, stop and classify pending/invalid rather than fabricating X.
4. **Evaluation.** A reviewer who did not issue J or execute the task compares the actual provider response to the frozen rubric, records inspected artifact hashes, identity, time, reasons, and disagreement if applicable. Only after this review may a staging E be submitted and treated as a candidate verified outcome. The current Evaluation service accepts internal provenance and nonempty refs without checking external truth; therefore its acceptance is **not** the independent verification. Its score effect must remain confined to isolated staging; no benchmark step writes a production E or reputation projection.
5. **Replay and publication.** Re-read O, J, X, E after process restart from the isolated durable store. Verify strict schemas, hashes, signed J against pinned public keys, exact parent pointers, subject/intent, state/time order, X proof body and finalized settlement, E evidence and reviewer authority. Reproduce deterministic J from the frozen pre-spend input and code/config; record any inability to reproduce. Freeze a redacted evidence manifest with artifact hashes, source coverage, reviewer records and explicit exclusions. Have a second independent reviewer sign the E1 conclusion.

## Stop rules and acceptance

Stop before any external transaction if provider identity, response inspectability, quote, route, wallet isolation, evidence freshness, capability, approvals, or caps fail. Stop after a transaction if settlement or outcome cannot be independently verified; preserve the ambiguous evidence and do not mark E1 complete. No retries that might double-spend without reconciling settlement and idempotency first. No production writes, billing activation, live Decisions spend influence, or IPX economics changes. A denied case is a safety observation, not an executed outcome. One verified loop demonstrates the mechanism for E1; it cannot estimate a false-ALLOW rate or satisfy E2/E3.
