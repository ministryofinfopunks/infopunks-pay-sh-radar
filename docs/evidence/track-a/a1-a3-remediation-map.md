## Dependency order

```text
A1: qualifying authority + quote-time decision context + accepted boundary
  ├── F1 context binding
  ├── F2 eligible execution authority / uniqueness
  ├── F4 immutable history snapshot boundary
  └── F6 explicit reassessment identity semantics
       ↓
A2: reviewable outcome evidence + scoped learning-route decision
  ├── F3 task rubric and reviewer provenance
  └── F5 explicitly choose paid pre-spend path; keep economic engine claim separate
       ↓
A3: complete discovery/export + independently verifiable tape and metrics
  └── F7 qualify, expose, and publish complete history
       ↓
Loop-001 real paid route experiment and independent replay
```

A1 context and eligibility must land together: publishing a history commitment without filtering out ineligible records cannot establish authority, and filtering without persisting the as-of boundary cannot establish what J2 knew. A2 needs the task artifact and a reproducible evaluation before a score delta can be treated as useful evidence. A3 consumes the qualified records and frozen context; it cannot repair their truth after the fact. Complete A1–A3 acceptance and a real, independently reviewed Loop-001 bundle are preconditions to a production GO.
