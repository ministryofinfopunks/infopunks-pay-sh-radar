  - region "Skill pack rules":
    - paragraph: Rules
    - heading "Infopunks-native doctrine." [level=2]
    - article:
      - heading "No receipt, no trust." [level=3]
      - paragraph: A run can recommend attention, but trust requires receipt-backed evidence.
    - article:
      - heading "Separate claim from evidence." [level=3]
      - paragraph: Claims must remain distinct from the artifacts that support or challenge them.
    - article:
      - heading "Unknown is a valid state." [level=3]
      - paragraph: Hermes should preserve unknowns instead of filling gaps with invented certainty.
    - article:
      - heading "Prefer do_not_use_yet over fake confidence." [level=3]
      - paragraph: When spend safety is unclear, block use until artifacts justify a narrower decision.
    - article:
      - heading "Every recommendation must cite artifacts." [level=3]
      - paragraph: Each decision should point to receipts, claims, loop runs, risk notes, scans, or skill traces.
    - article:
      - heading "Decision states are bounded." [level=3]
      - paragraph: All judgments must map to trust, caution, do_not_use_yet, unproven, or disputed.
    - article:
      - heading "Pre-spend judgment comes first." [level=3]
      - paragraph: Investigate before payment, route selection, provider trust, or claim amplification.
