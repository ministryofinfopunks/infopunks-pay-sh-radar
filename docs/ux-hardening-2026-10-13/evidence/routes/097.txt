  - paragraph: Platform Risk Categories
  - heading "Origin confusion gets its own watch" [level=2]
  - article:
    - heading "Duplicate ticker" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Same-name claims require exact-contract review before identity is inferred.
  - article:
    - heading "Impersonator" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Branding and social resemblance are signals to verify, not a misconduct finding.
  - article:
    - heading "Vampire / copycat risk" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Similarity, migration, or brand-adjacent claims remain review cues, not a conclusion about intent.
  - article:
    - heading "Fake relaunch risk" [level=3]
    - paragraph: A relaunch claim needs canonical contracts, source timestamps, and reviewed origin links.
  - article:
    - heading "Launchpad displacement risk" [level=3]
    - paragraph: When launch surfaces fragment, copies, fake relaunches, and origin confusion can spread across venues. Track the exact launch origin and source timestamp.
  - article:
    - heading "Duplicate social claim" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Social resemblance or repeated handles must be matched to a canonical source before identity is inferred.
  - article:
    - heading "Liquidity claim unverified" [level=3]
    - paragraph: A pool, screenshot, or volume claim does not establish reserves, depth, or exit reliability.
  - article:
    - heading "Creator-fee claim uncertain" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Fee terms require primary terms and a dated receipt before they are promoted.
  - article:
    - heading "Fee-model claim unverified" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Fee-model changes require primary terms or on-chain evidence before promotion.
  - article:
    - heading "Burn / buyback claim unverified" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Burn and buyback claims require transaction-level evidence before they become desk memory.
  - article:
    - heading "Creator-revenue claim uncertain" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Creator-revenue routing claims remain uncertain until primary terms or on-chain routing evidence exists.
  - article:
    - heading "Front-end dependency risk" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Interface availability and canonical launch origin can diverge; status alone is not proof.
  - article:
    - heading "Direct Uniswap low-liquidity risk" [level=3]
    - paragraph: A direct pool does not establish reliable liquidity, identity, or safety.
  - paragraph: How Infopunks Flags Risk
  - heading "Signals that require a slower read" [level=2]
  - article:
    - heading "Repeated ticker" [level=3]
    - paragraph: The same ticker appears against more than one disclosed contract; it remains unresolved until manual review.
  - article:
    - heading "Unverifiable launch source" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Launch claims without a reviewable contract, pair, or source link remain context, not identity.
  - article:
    - heading "Thin liquidity" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Limited or unverified pool evidence can make surface activity unreliable.
  - article:
    - heading "Suspicious deployer" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Deployer history or clustered claims are verification gates, not a definitive finding.
  - article:
    - heading "Fake social links" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Social links must be source-checked; a link alone does not establish a canonical channel.
  - article:
    - heading "Conflicting contract claims" [level=3]
    - paragraph: Different contract claims for one identity remain unresolved until receipts align.
  - paragraph: Improve the record
  - heading "Bring risk evidence, not hype." [level=2]
  - paragraph: Submit contracts, launch links, explorer receipts, liquidity context, or conflicting-identity evidence for manual review.
