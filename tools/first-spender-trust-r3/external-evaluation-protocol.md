# External economic truth — offline intake protocol v1

**HOLD. No external corpus has been received; no new economic result exists.** This protocol accepts externally curated archives only. It does not acquire data, call providers, run a payment, issue a capability, execute an agent strategy, publish proofs or authorize an experiment. `intake.mjs` verifies signed intake/decision/reveal documents and computes transparent accounting. Policy execution and custody require independently administered offline environments described below. Software tests are fictional structural vectors, never economic measurements.

## People, custody and permitted evidence

An independently appointed curator controls case selection and archive collection. A separate methodology reviewer approves the sampling/cost/value plan before holdout use. A custodian separately controls the withheld outcome/value/cost archive and signs each stage in an append-only, independently witnessed custody log. The decision producer cannot administer that store or receive labels. Independent outcome, value and cost reviewers authenticate the evidence and sign its reveal; none may be the decision producer or curator. The tool enforces distinct registry organization IDs for these roles, but cannot establish real-world independence from labels. Independently verified identities, conflicts, authority and evidence permissions must be recorded in the approval register and trust registry before acceptance. Separate local processes/agent reviews under the same operator are insufficient.

Registry roles: `curator`, `methodology-reviewer`, `custodian`, `decision-producer`, `outcome-assessor`, `value-reviewer`, `cost-reviewer`. Each Ed25519 key has an exact grant `{bundle_id}`; registry pin is delivered out of band by the independent review owner. No shipped registry contains production identities. Registry/key expiry or revocation blocks intake; a pin is not a live revocation oracle. Independently approve its capture/currentness at each stage. Signatures use the same versioned canonical encoding/domain as provenance, with the corresponding role in the domain. Documents are exactly `{body, signatures: {role: {key_id, signature_base64}}}`.

Private evidence is stored by raw byte SHA-256 under `archive/<64-hex-digest>`. The CLI reads regular local files, verifies every referenced artifact, never retrieves URLs or runs the contents, and outputs hashes and ledgers without raw evidence. Source agreements must permit private evaluation; the public proof/publication boundary stays closed. Artifact integrity and signatures establish custody and attestation, not delivery truth on their own. The independent assessors must examine provider identity, capture, source contracts, content/delivery correctness and cost/value validity. A malicious or mistaken trusted reviewer remains a risk requiring independent audits and conflict review.

## Preregistration and information boundary

Before curating holdout, independently preregister intent-to-treat population, provider eligibility, time window, task/deliverable contracts, sampling frame, exclusion reasons, cluster units, sample size/power rationale, adverse outcomes, missing-data rules, discovery budget, stop conditions, estimand and confidence procedure. Keep all offered/declined/failed/disputed tasks and fees. Group repeated provider/task/incident cases by cluster across splits; do not treat correlated rows as independent. The tool rejects the same cluster in development and heldout; reviewers must prevent disguised overlapping provider/task/time clusters. Publish no market conclusion from a balanced stress set or a single purchase.

Freeze valuation rule and independent buyer utility/risk assessment **before** outcome inspection or decisions: bounded successful-delivery value, failure downside, time sensitivity, currency/minor-unit scale and FX method/evidence if needed. Freeze cost accounting categories/rates, measurement method, zero-cost evidence requirements and shared setup amortization. Actual invoices and observations must support costs; authored toy prices and free probe availability are not external evidence. No hindsight revaluation of a blocked profitable task is allowed.

All six strategies are non-executable, frozen offline preferences: `abstention`, `no_radar` under common admissibility, `static`, `cheap_checks`, `preflight`, `full_radar`. The sample plan defines actual feasible cheap features and their access costs before labels. Preflight may reject or request a full judgment; its preference in this accounting protocol must never be imported as an execution decision. Full Radar replay must use the exact frozen canonical policy, signed judgment/context/evidence and separate no-execution evidence domain. A bounded test verdict is not full-purchase permission and must abstain in this full-purchase estimand. A distinct bounded-test estimand would need separate preregistration.

Pin source revision, strategy byte digests (including config/model/prompt/runtime/seeds), source/evidence/valuation/cost schema versions and source manifests. Full Radar must document its **additional** features/evidence beyond preflight, how they were known at decision time, who independently verified them and actual acquisition/latency cost. A larger prompt over identical evidence is not independently established incremental assurance. No inference from the case ID/condition ordering may disclose truth; randomize opaque identifiers under custodian control.

## Stage 1 — registration, before decisions

Registration body is exactly:

```text
version = infopunks.external-eval.registration.v1
bundle_id, created_at, decision_cutoff, source_revision (40-hex commit)
protocol_hash, sample_plan_hash, valuation_rule_hash, cost_rule_hash
currency (ISO-style three-uppercase code), unit_scale (0..18)
strategy_hashes = {abstention, no_radar, static, cheap_checks, preflight, full_radar}
cases[], truth_commitment
```

All `_hash` fields use `sha256:<64 lowercase hex>`. Protocol/plan/rules/strategy and case evidence hashes must resolve to actual private archive bytes. Each case is exactly `{case_id, provider_id, cluster_id, split, available_at, admissible, quote_evidence_hash, observation_evidence_hash, cheap_features_hash, full_features_hash, valuation_evidence_hash}`. `split` is development or heldout; availability must be no later than registration. Strict field rejection prevents adding outcome columns to this intake. The decision producer receives only approved pre-decision projections, not the full evidence archive, labels, delivery truth or cost reveal. Blob contents also require independent leakage review: a hash-only field schema cannot prove a feature blob is free of hindsight.

Custodian commits `truth_commitment = hash({salt, truth})` using 32 random secret bytes encoded as 64 hex characters. Keep salt/truth private until after independently witnessed decision freeze. Sign registration by curator, methodology reviewer and custodian from three distinct independently established organizations. A local commit timestamp or file hash alone does not prove ordering. The custodian's separately witnessed log must prove actual access and stage ordering; signed self-reported timestamps are not enough. No tuning or policy/config changes after holdout access. A protocol/policy change retires the holdout and requires fresh independent curation and preregistration.

## Stage 2 — freeze all decisions, without labels

Decision document body: `{version: infopunks.external-eval.decisions.v1, bundle_id, registration_hash, created_at, rows}`. `registration_hash` hashes the complete signed registration. Every registered case has exactly one row per six arms, including abstentions and failures. Each row is exactly `{case_id, arm, preference, reason, strategy_hash, evidence_hash, full_verdict, execution_authorized:false}`. Preference is select or abstain; inadmissible cases and abstention arm must abstain. Only full Radar has a non-null canonical verdict. Select requires full Radar verdict proceed; full refusal/insufficient evidence/bounded test cannot be promoted by later outcomes. Missing, extra or duplicate cases/arms and changed strategy digests fail validation.

Evidence hash resolves to a private decision trace binding exact available feature digests, canonical judgment and provenance checks, code/runtime/config/seeds, quoted fee, latency and reason. Independent methodology review must inspect this trace; intake does not itself reimplement or certify canonical judgments. Decision-producer signature and the **same custodian key** countersign all rows, bound to the signed registration. Custodian records the freeze in its independent log before granting outcome access. Stage input schema excludes reveal; freeze can run with no truth file present. The tool is an auditable file boundary, not an OS security sandbox or an independently witnessed secrecy system.

## Stage 3 — reveal and complete accounting

Reveal body: `{version: infopunks.external-eval.reveal.v1, bundle_id, registration_hash, freeze_hash, revealed_at, salt, truth:{rows}}`. Both complete signed documents and secret commitment must match. Outcome, value and cost reviewers plus same custodian sign the whole reveal. Those four organizations must be distinct; outcome/value/cost reviewers must also be independent of curator and decision producer. Signatures authenticate the evidence claims; named independent approvals still remain required. Reviewer-role identifiers in test fixtures never count as approval.

For every case and arm, truth row is exactly `{case_id, arm, outcome, outcome_evidence_hash, earned_minor, downside_minor, value_evidence_hash, costs}`. Outcome is verified_success, verified_failure, disputed or missing_counterfactual. A verified result needs actual outcome evidence and independently supported value/downside evidence. A selected-but-unverified outcome has null value/downside and null net. An unperformed alternative is `missing_counterfactual`, never an invented delivery/success label. Even abstained cases retain that unknown hypothetical outcome; known abstention utility is zero **before** its incurred check costs, without pretending delivery was observed. Retrospective outcome replay is valid only where the preregistered replay/transportability assumptions are independently defensible. Existing provider outcomes do not automatically supply all strategy counterfactuals.

Every row includes all twelve separate costs: service, judgment, cheap_check, preflight, inference, verification, payment, gas, infrastructure, latency, recovery, refund. Each is exactly `{kind, amount_minor, evidence_hash, missing_reason}`. Amount is a nonnegative integer string in registered common minor units or null. Known zero needs evidence; unknown needs a reason and null evidence. Refund is a separately evidenced credit, never assumed from a complaint. Costs of a refusal, timeout, failed judgment, partial delivery and later recovery stay with that case and arm. Shared/setup costs require preregistered allocation, and original total plus allocation rationale in the cost evidence; do not disappear them in a zero per-request overhead. Different currencies cannot be pooled without frozen independently evidenced conversion. Unpriced latency remains unknown, with timing facts in the trace.

This is an **offline archive** protocol: outcome and cost truth must already be independently collected and sealed before decisions. Later run-dependent invoices, unmeasured counterfactual costs or genuinely unavailable values are recorded as null. A new future measurement cannot be backfilled into the frozen commitment; it requires a new version and independent review with declared exposure. No new purchase is authorized to fill a counterfactual.

The report keeps every row, missing counterfactual, each cost and paired delta separately. Net equals earned value minus downside minus incurred costs plus evidenced refunds; abstention has no delivered value/downside. Any unknown required cost or selected outcome leaves net and paired deltas null. Development and heldout summaries are separate; no complete-case filtering, imputation or pooling. `economic_usefulness` stays NOT_ESTABLISHED: software acceptance is not approval, causality, calibration, a market confidence interval or proof of willingness to pay. Report publication remains prohibited.

## Reproduce with independently supplied private inputs

Use Node 24.21.0 from an exact clean R3 checkout. The registry pin and evaluation time come from the authorized independent review owner, never auto-derived from the submitted registry:

```sh
node tools/first-spender-trust-r3/intake.mjs register registration-input.json "$REVIEW_REGISTRY_SHA" "$REVIEW_UTC" private-archive registration-lock.json
node tools/first-spender-trust-r3/intake.mjs freeze decisions-input.json "$REVIEW_REGISTRY_SHA" "$REVIEW_UTC" private-archive decisions-lock.json
node tools/first-spender-trust-r3/intake.mjs evaluate revealed-input.json "$REVIEW_REGISTRY_SHA" "$REVIEW_UTC" private-archive report.json
```

Inputs respectively have exactly `{registry,registration}`, `{registry,registration,freeze}` and `{registry,registration,freeze,reveal}`. Outputs are exclusively created with private file mode and are never silently overwritten. Empty/missing registry, evidence, signatures or cases fail closed with nonzero exit and HOLD. Archive hashes, signed custody sequence and current registry snapshots must be retained privately for independent reproduction. No external input or registry is fabricated as a runnable demonstration here. Required independent statistical analysis, representativeness assessment, fee evidence and approval remain outstanding after tool acceptance.
